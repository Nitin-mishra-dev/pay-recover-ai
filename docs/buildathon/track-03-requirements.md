# Razorpay AI Buildathon 2026 — Track 03 Requirements & Submission Criteria

This document records the specific requirements, judging rubrics, and deadlines for Track 03 (AI Revenue Recovery).

---

## 1. Official Track 03 Problem Statement
> *"Build an agent that detects revenue at risk, determines the right intervention, and executes a bounded recovery workflow: from payment failures and checkout abandonment to overdue receivables."*
> 
> **The Official Bar**: *"Show measured money recovered with stopping rules and a full audit trail."*

---

## 2. Hard Constraints & Milestones
* **Submission Deadline**: September 5, 2026.
* **Format**:
  1. Public GitHub Repository with clean code, setup scripts, and tests.
  2. 5-Minute Pitch & Demo Video demonstrating both happy path and failure/safety handling ("What broke & how we got out").
  3. Clear System Architecture & Economic Optimization Diagram.
* **Evaluation Criteria**:
  * Real measured rupees recovered vs baseline across a batch.
  * Deterministic stopping rules and safety invariants.
  * Immutable, transparent audit trail.
  * Technical depth and defensibility.
