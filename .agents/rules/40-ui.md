# PAYRECOVER UI STANDARD

The UI exists to communicate business and decision quality.

Do not build generic "AI SaaS" decoration.

The UI must answer:

1. How much revenue is at risk?
2. How much was recovered?
3. What action is proposed?
4. Why?
5. What is the expected value?
6. Which safety checks passed?
7. What happened?
8. What does the benchmark prove?

## Priority screens

1. Overview
2. Recovery Queue
3. Decision Room
4. Audit Trail
5. Benchmark
6. Safety Controls

## Decision Room

Show:
- amount
- failure
- recoverability
- candidate actions
- action economics
- selected action
- merchant policy
- safety checks
- execution state

## Avoid

- unnecessary gradients
- fake AI animations
- meaningless chat interfaces
- excessive modal dialogs
- decorative graphs without business meaning

The strongest screen is the decision screen.
